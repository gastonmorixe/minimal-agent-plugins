Emit `%%sideband:open interleave-thinking%%` spans for private reasoning while you draft. The body is your private voice, written plainly, and dropped before the user sees the answer.

Use a span when a first pass suggests a second look. Use a span when you are about to commit to a claim with real doubt. Use a span between steps of a long task.

Do not use a span for primary reasoning on a hard task. Use pre-response thinking for that. Do not use a span to pad the answer. Do not put text the user must read inside a span.
